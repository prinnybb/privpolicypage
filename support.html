Support

Playing

How does the daily puzzle work?

Every day there’s a new palette of five colors, the same for everyone. Each color shows for three seconds, then you rebuild it with the hue, saturation and brightness sliders. Each color scores up to 10 points, so the best possible day is 50. A new palette arrives at midnight in your time zone.

How is my score calculated?

Hueman measures the difference between the real color and yours using CIEDE2000, a formula designed to match how people actually perceive color differences. A tiny difference you can barely see scores close to 10.

What’s an endless run?

In an endless run, colors keep coming and every round is a little harder: the color shows for less time and you need a closer match. Miss the target score and you lose a heart. Lose all three hearts and the run is over.

How do I unlock modes and games?

Every game you finish earns XP. As you level up you unlock Odd One Out (level 2), Blitz (3), Gradient Sort (4), Subtle (5), the daily archive (6), Precision (7) and more. Your current level and the next unlock are shown on the Play tab.

How do challenges with friends work?

After any game, tap “Challenge a friend” to send a code like HUE-XXXXX-XXXXX. Your friend enters it under Play → “Enter a challenge code” and plays the exact same colors. Challenges are always playable, even for modes they haven’t unlocked yet.

Your data

Do I need an account?

No. Hueman has no accounts. Everything is saved on your phone.

I got a new phone. Can I move my progress?

Progress is stored on your device only, so it doesn’t transfer automatically to a new phone. Restoring from a full device backup (iCloud or Google) usually brings it back.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings → Reset all progress. This deletes your games, streaks, levels and rivals from the device and can’t be undone.

The daily reminder doesn’t arrive.

Check that notifications are allowed for Hueman in your phone’s Settings, then turn the reminder off and on again in Hueman → Settings.
