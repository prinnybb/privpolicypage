Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

The short version
  • You don’t need an account, and we don’t ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your games, scores, levels and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We don’t collect, sell or share personal data, and we don’t track you across apps or websites.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

This policy explains how the Hueman mobile app (“Hueman”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. It applies to the app on iOS and Android and to this website.

Information stored on your device

To work, Hueman saves the following data locally on your device, using the phone’s standard app storage:

  • Games you’ve played, your scores, streaks, levels and experience points.
  • Your settings, such as theme, accent color, haptics and reminder time.
  • An optional display name you type yourself, used only on share cards and challenges you choose to send.

This data never leaves your device unless you share something yourself (see below). We have no servers that receive it, and we can’t see it.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal data. Hueman has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The current version of the app does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services and does not show ads.

Sharing you choose to do

When you tap “Share”, Hueman opens your phone’s standard share sheet with a result card or text that you can send to the apps and people you pick. Challenge codes contain only the game, the board and a score. If you’ve set a display name, it’s included in the text you choose to share. What happens after you share is governed by the app you share with.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled by your phone itself (a “local” notification). No push service or server is involved, and no notification token is sent to us.

Children

Hueman is a general-audience game. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Deleting your data

You can erase all game data at any time in the app under Settings → Reset all progress, or by deleting the app. Because we don’t hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, the CCPA in California, or Chile’s data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since Hueman does not collect personal data, there is no data held by us to request — but you’re welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Hueman changes how data is handled — for example by adding optional advertising or an in-app purchase — we will update this page before that version is released and change the “Last updated” date above.
